# The SOP an AI agent can follow

```
AGENT:     one name, one job
TRIGGER:   when it runs (schedule or event)
READS:     exactly what it may look at
DOES:      the steps, in order, in plain words
DONE:      a counter or a file, never an adjective
NEVER:     the lines it must not cross
SEND:      fully autonomous / human-assisted / human-led
REPORTS:   to which stage, and what line goes in the daily brief
```

## A filled example: Qualifier (stage 3 of a lead engine)
- **TRIGGER:** after the Enricher finishes, weekdays from 07:00.
- **READS:** the enriched lead sheet and the ideal-customer rubric. Nothing else.
- **DOES:** score each lead against the rubric 0–100; write one line on why; mark anti-signals (for example, already has an internal AI team).
- **DONE:** every researched lead has a score and a reason.
- **NEVER:** contact anyone; change the rubric; score a lead whose signal is older than 21 days as fresh.
- **SEND:** fully autonomous (wrong costs a minute, and the next stage catches it).
- **REPORTS:** the qualified list to the Opener; "N qualified" in the morning brief.

**The rule behind the rules:** if a new human hire couldn't do the job from this page on their first morning, an agent can't either.
